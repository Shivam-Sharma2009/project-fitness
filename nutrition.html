🥗 NUTRITION GUIDE

Eat Healthy, Live Better

Good nutrition is the foundation of a healthy and active lifestyle.

Essential Nutrients

🍗 Protein

Protein helps build and repair muscles. Sources: Eggs, Chicken, Fish, Paneer, Milk, Soybean.

🍚 Carbohydrates

Carbs provide energy for daily activities. Sources: Rice, Bread, Oats, Potato, Fruits.

🥑 Healthy Fats

Healthy fats support brain and heart health. Sources: Nuts, Seeds, Avocado, Olive Oil.

🥦 Vitamins & Minerals

Boost immunity and support body functions. Sources: Fruits, Vegetables and Dairy Products.

🍽 Sample Daily Meal Plan

Breakfast: Oats + Milk + Banana

Lunch: Rice + Dal + Vegetables + Salad

Evening Snack: Fruits + Nuts

Dinner: Roti + Paneer/Chicken + Vegetables

💡 Nutrition Tips

✅ Drink 2-3 litres of water daily.

✅ Eat fresh fruits and vegetables.

✅ Avoid excessive junk food.

✅ Include protein in every meal.

✅ Do not skip breakfast.

✅ Limit sugary drinks and snacks.

"Take care of your body. It’s the only place you have to live."
