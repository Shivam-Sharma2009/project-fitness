What you can say

  • "What should I do today?" — proactive coaching + guidance
  • "Login" — voice login
  • "Start workout" / "Next exercise" — adaptive voice-and-visual workout guidance
  • "Show progress" — hear and see your steps and workout progress
  • "Read leaderboard" — voice leaderboard
  • "Join challenge" — a challenge suited to you
  • "Check my Fitness DNA" — personalized recommendation
  • "Go home" / "Open fitness" / "Open nutrition" — voice navigation
  • "Turn on high contrast" / "Make the text bigger" / "Turn off captions" — voice control of the page itself
  • "About" — hear the project pitch
  • "Help" — hear this list again

Keyboard shortcut: press Space anywhere on the page (outside a button) to start step tracking.
